The situation

This time, the same topic — “How to Prevent Cyber Attacks in AWS Cloud” — is presented by a team of four. Each member takes one part, and you need clean handover phrases between speakers.

2

Splitting the presentation into 4 roles

One clear way to divide the same AWS Cloud Security topic across a team of four.

Member 1 — Introduction
Greets the audience, introduces the team, and outlines what each member will cover.
Member 2 — Identity & Access
Covers IAM, least privilege, and multi-factor authentication.
Member 3 — Network Security
Covers Security Groups, firewalls, and VPC design.
Member 4 — Monitoring & Closing
Covers CloudTrail/GuardDuty, then summarizes and opens Q&A.
3

Full team script, with handovers

Notice the handover phrase each member uses to pass the presentation to the next person.

4

Handover phrases

These phrases are the glue that makes a team presentation sound smooth and rehearsed.

Phrase / WordMeaning (Sinhala)Example
I'll now hand over to...දැන් මම ...ට හරවනවා“I'll now hand over to my teammate for the next part.”
Thanks, [name]. On my part...ස්තූතියි, [නම]. මගේ කොටසින්...“Thanks, Roshan. On my part, I'll cover monitoring.”
Now, over to [name] for...දැන්, [නම] ...ට“Now, over to Dinithi for the conclusion.”
as a teamකණ්ඩායමක් විදිහට“As a team, we researched this topic together.”
5

Grammar focus

The exact patterns that make team handovers sound smooth.

'I'll now hand over to...' — handover patternPresentation skill

This fixed phrase (will + hand over to + name) is the standard way to pass a team presentation from one speaker to the next.

  • I'll now hand over to Sithumi.දැන් සිතුමිට හරවනවා.
  • Now, over to Dinithi for the conclusion.දැන් දිනිතිට හරවනවා.
First person plural — 'we' for team ownershipGrammar point

Use ‘we’ (not ‘I’) when talking about the team's work as a whole, and switch to ‘I’ only for your own individual part.

  • We're a team of four presenting on...අපි... presentation කරන කණ්ඩායමක්.
  • On my part, I'll cover monitoring.මගේ කොටසින්, monitoring cover කරනවා.
Thanking + continuing — 'Thanks, [name]. On the network side...'Fixed pattern

Briefly thank the previous speaker by name before starting your own section — this keeps the presentation feeling connected.

  • Thanks, Sithumi. On the network side...ස්තූතියි, සිතුමි. network පැත්තෙන්...
6

Common mistakes

Errors that make a team presentation sound disconnected between speakers.

Common mistakeCorrect versionWhy
I hand over to Sithumi now.I'll now hand over to Sithumi.Use the fixed future form ‘I'll now hand over to...’ — it's the standard presentation phrase.
I am presenting on IAM.I'll cover IAM. / On my part, I'll talk about IAM.When describing your assigned section, ‘I'll cover/talk about’ fits a team-handover context better than ‘I am presenting on’.
We is a team of four.We are a team of four.‘We’ always takes ‘are’, never ‘is’.
Now over Dinithi for conclusion.Now, over to Dinithi for the conclusion.Don't drop ‘to’ in the handover phrase ‘over to [name]’.
