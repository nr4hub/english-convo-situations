The situation

You're at Hikkaduwa or Maradana station and need to buy a ticket, ask about the platform, or check the train timing.

2

Key phrases

Everything you need to buy a ticket and check train details.

  • One ticket to Maradana, please.මරදානට ටිකට් එකක් කරුණාකර.
  • Is this a second-class or third-class ticket?මේක second class ද third class ද?
  • What time does the next train leave?ලබන දුම්රිය පිටත් වෙන්නේ කීයටද?
  • Which platform does the Colombo train leave from?කොළඹට යන දුම්රිය පිටත් වෙන්නේ මොන platform එකෙන්ද?
  • Is this train running on time today?අද මේ දුම්රිය වේලාවට ධාවනය වෙනවද?
3

Sample conversation

A full ticket-counter exchange at Hikkaduwa station.

At the ticket counter, Hikkaduwa station
You
Good morning, one ticket to Maradana, please.සුබ උදෑසනක්, මරදානට ටිකට් එකක් කරුණාකර.
Clerk
Second class or third class?Second class ද third class ද?
You
Second class, please. How much is it?Second class, කරුණාකර. කීයද?
Clerk
That's one hundred and ten rupees.රුපියල් එකසිය දහයයි.
You
Here you go. Also, what time does the next train leave?මෙන්න. ඒවගේම, ලබන දුම්රිය පිටත් වෙන්නේ කීයටද?
Clerk
It leaves at 5:10, from platform one.5.10ට පිටත් වෙනවා, platform එකෙන්.
You
Thank you. Is it running on time today?ස්තූතියි. අද වේලාවට ධාවනය වෙනවද?
Clerk
Yes, it's on time so far.ඔව්, මේ දක්වා වේලාවට තියෙනවා.

Words used at train stations across Sri Lanka.

5

Grammar focus

Question forms for timetables, tickets, and current train status.

'What time does...' — present simple questionsCore structure

Timetables are fixed schedules, so ask about them with present simple + ‘does’, not ‘will’.

  • What time does the next train leave?ලබන දුම්රිය කීයටද පිටත් වෙන්නේ?
'One ticket to...' — shopping-style requestsFixed pattern

A short, direct pattern used for buying tickets — no need for a full sentence.

  • One ticket to Maradana, please.මරදානට ටිකට් එකක් කරුණාකර.
Present continuous for current statusGrammar point

Use present continuous to ask about the train's status right now — on time or delayed.

  • Is it running on time today?අද ඒක වේලාවට ධාවනය වෙනවද?
6

Common mistakes

Small errors that come up often when asking about schedules.

Common mistakeCorrect versionWhy
What time the next train leave?What time does the next train leave?Don't forget ‘does’ when forming a question in the present simple.
Give me one ticket Maradana.One ticket to Maradana, please.Add the preposition ‘to’ before the destination, and ‘please’ for politeness.
Is train on time today?Is the train on time today?Don't drop the article ‘the’ before a specific noun.
It leaves on 5:10.It leaves at 5:10.Use ‘at’ with exact clock times, not ‘on’.
